# General Booking FAQ

## Check-in and check-out times
Standard check-in is 3:00 PM local hotel time on the arrival date. Check-out is
11:00 AM. All cancellation deadlines are measured against the 3:00 PM check-in
time on the arrival date.

## Which rate plan do I have?
The rate plan is shown on the booking confirmation and in the customer-service
chat when the agent looks up the reservation. The three plans are Flexible,
Semi-Flexible, and Non-Refundable. Each has its own cancellation policy
document.

## Can I change the dates of my booking?
Flexible and Semi-Flexible bookings can be changed subject to availability and
any rate difference. Non-Refundable bookings cannot be changed.

## Modifying the number of guests
The number of guests can be updated free of charge up to check-in, as long as
it stays within the room's maximum occupancy.

## Force majeure
If a government travel ban, natural disaster, or hotel closure prevents the
stay, guests are entitled to a full refund regardless of rate plan. These
cases are handled as a manual review; the chat agent will collect details and
open a request.

## Group bookings
Reservations of 5 rooms or more are treated as group bookings and follow a
separate contract; the standard rate-plan policies above do not apply.

## Contacting a human
The customer-service chat can escalate to a human agent if the guest asks. Wait
times are usually under 10 minutes during business hours.
