# Semi-Flexible Rate — Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Overview
The Semi-Flexible rate sits between Flexible and Non-Refundable. It is priced
about 10% below the Flexible rate and is a good choice for guests who are
fairly sure of their travel dates but want some protection.

## Full refund window
A Semi-Flexible booking is **fully refundable (room charge plus prepaid taxes
and fees)** when cancelled **7 days (168 hours) or more before check-in**.

## Partial refund window
If the guest cancels **between 48 hours and 7 days before check-in**, they
receive a **50% refund of the room charge**. All prepaid taxes and fees are
still refunded in full.

## Inside 48 hours
If the guest cancels **less than 48 hours before check-in**, the **room charge
is forfeited entirely**. Only prepaid taxes and fees are returned.

## No-show
A no-show on the Semi-Flexible rate forfeits the room charge; taxes and fees
are refunded within 7 days.

## Examples
- Cancel a 400 USD, 4-night Semi-Flexible booking 3 days out: 50% room refund,
  refund 200.00 USD.
- Cancel the same booking 9 days out: full refund of 400.00 USD.
- Cancel the same booking 12 hours out: room forfeited, only taxes refunded.
