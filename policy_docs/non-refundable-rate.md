# Non-Refundable Rate — Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Overview
The Non-Refundable (promo) rate is the cheapest fare, typically 20% below the
Flexible rate. In exchange for the discount the room charge cannot be refunded
under any cancellation timing.

## What is refundable
If the guest cancels a Non-Refundable booking, the **room charge is never
returned**, regardless of how far in advance the cancellation is made.
**Prepaid taxes and fees are always refunded**, because the hotel does not
remit those to the tax authority for a stay that did not happen.

## No-show
A no-show on the Non-Refundable rate is treated the same as a cancellation:
room charge forfeited, taxes and fees refunded within 7 days.

## Date changes
Non-Refundable bookings cannot be modified or moved to new dates. The guest
would need to cancel (forfeiting the room charge) and make a new booking.

## Exceptions
Documented medical emergencies and government travel bans may qualify for a
goodwill refund at the hotel manager's discretion. The chat agent cannot grant
these automatically; it will raise a manual review request.

## Example
- Cancel a 335 USD Non-Refundable booking (300 room + 35 tax) at any time:
  refund 35.00 USD (taxes only).
