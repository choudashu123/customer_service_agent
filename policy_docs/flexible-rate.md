# Flexible Rate — Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Overview
The Flexible rate is our most lenient fare. It carries the highest nightly
price but gives guests the widest cancellation window. It is the default rate
offered at checkout unless the guest chooses a cheaper, more restrictive plan.

## Free cancellation window
A booking on the Flexible rate can be cancelled for a **full refund of the
entire amount paid (room charge plus prepaid taxes and fees)** as long as the
cancellation is made **48 hours or more before the check-in time** (check-in
time is 3:00 PM local hotel time on the arrival date).

## Cancellations inside 48 hours
If the guest cancels **less than 48 hours before check-in**, a **one-night
penalty** equal to one night's room rate is retained by the hotel. The
remainder of the room charge is refunded, and all prepaid taxes and fees are
refunded in full. The penalty never exceeds the total amount paid.

## No-show
If the guest does not arrive and does not cancel, the booking is treated as a
same-day cancellation: the one-night penalty applies and the balance is
refunded automatically within 7 days.

## How to cancel
Guests can cancel through the customer-service chat by providing their booking
reference (for example HTL-101) and the surname on the reservation. The agent
will quote the exact refund before anything is charged or cancelled, and will
ask the guest to confirm.

## Examples
- Cancel a 450 USD, 3-night Flexible booking 10 days out: refund 450.00 USD (100%).
- Cancel a 600 USD, 3-night Flexible booking 26 hours out (rate 200/night):
  one-night penalty of 200.00 USD is kept, refund 400.00 USD.
